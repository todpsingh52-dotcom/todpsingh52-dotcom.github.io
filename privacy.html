Privacy policy

DadPhone Backup is used by one person on one Android phone. This page describes how that app handles Google user data.

The app reads sound recordings stored on that phone and uploads new recordings to a folder in the phone owner's Google Drive. The Google permission used is drive.file. That permission lets the app create and update only the files the app itself places in Drive.

Recordings stay in the owner's Google account. The app does not sell them, does not share them with another company, and does not use them for advertising.

The phone keeps its own copy of each recording. Removing a file from the phone does not delete the Drive copy.
